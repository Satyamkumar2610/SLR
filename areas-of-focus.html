Areas of Focus

Five domains. One mandate.

The Centre analyses how evolving configurations of power, capital, trade, energy, regulation, and technology are reshaping economies, industries, and institutions worldwide.

01

Global Law & Cross-Border Regulation

The evolving legal and regulatory architecture that governs international commerce, investment, technology, sanctions, and cross-border transactions across fragmented jurisdictions. The Centre tracks how diverging regulatory regimes, sanctions architectures, and cross-border enforcement mechanisms are reshaping the legal frameworks within which businesses and states operate.

Explore This Area →
02

Geoeconomics & Strategic Affairs

The strategic deployment of trade, investment, finance, technology, resources, and economic policy as instruments of statecraft, economic security, and international influence. The Centre analyses how major powers use economic tools to project influence, secure strategic advantages, and reshape the international order.

Explore This Area →
03

Global Finance & Investment

The global financial architecture, sovereign and institutional capital flows, economic restrictions, and the deepening nexus between capital allocation and geopolitical risk. The Centre examines how investment patterns, financial sanctions, and capital controls are redefining the relationship between markets and geopolitics.

Explore This Area →
04

Energy & Energy Transitions

Energy security, global markets, critical minerals, transition finance, and the reconfiguration of global energy systems impacting industrial competitiveness. The Centre analyses how energy transition pathways, critical mineral dependencies, and shifting energy geographies create both risks and opportunities for states and industries.

Explore This Area →
05

Emerging Technologies & Governance

Artificial intelligence, semiconductors, digital economies, autonomous systems, cyber policy, technology governance, and nascent cross-border regulatory frameworks. The Centre tracks how technology competition, governance gaps, and emerging regulatory paradigms are reshaping industries and international relations.

Explore This Area →
