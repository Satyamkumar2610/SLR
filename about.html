About the Centre

Rigorous analysis. Actionable intelligence.

The SLR Centre for Global Law, Geoeconomics & Emerging Technologies is a high-level research, intelligence, and strategic advisory centre anchored in India with a global mandate.

Our Mission

Navigating the forces reshaping the world

The Centre exists to equip senior decision-makers — in business, government, and international organisations — with the depth of analysis and clarity of intelligence required to navigate an era of accelerating geopolitical complexity.

We operate at the intersection of law, economics, and emerging technology, producing research and advisory services that are substantive, not reactive — designed to anticipate, not merely respond.

Our work spans five interconnected research domains across seven priority sectors, anchored in India and extending across eight geographies and the strategic corridors connecting them.

"Situated at the nexus of law, geoeconomics, and emerging technologies, the Centre provides the analytical depth and strategic foresight that consequential decisions demand."

— SLR Centre for Global Law, Geoeconomics & Emerging Technologies
